💪 Shoulder Health Mini-Routine (Pre-Volleyball)
▼

Do this before every volleyball session. Keeps your shoulders healthy and primed.

  • Band External Rotations — 15 reps/side (light band, elbow tucked)
  • Band Pull-Aparts — 20 reps (keep arms straight, retract scaps)
  • Arm Circles — 20 reps (forward then backward, controlled)
  • Scap Pushups — 15 reps (body plank, only move shoulder blades)
